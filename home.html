    Main branches of chemistry

    Organic Chemistry

    Explore the fascinating world of carbon-based compounds that form the foundation of life. Learn how molecules interact to create everything from medicines to materials.

    Inorganic Chemistry

    Explore deep into the study of metals, minerals, and non-carbon compounds. Understand how inorganic reactions shape industries, catalysis, and environmental processes.

    Soil Chemistry

    Discover how chemical interactions within soil affect plant growth, nutrient cycles, and sustainability. Learn to analyze and improve soil health for agriculture and ecology.

    Physical Chemistry

    Uncover the principles that link chemistry and physics — from thermodynamics to quantum mechanics. Explore how energy and matter interact at the molecular level.
